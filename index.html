FAQ

good questions, honest answers
How much does a game cost?

You could see our base price in the rate card, because a game doesn't has fixed price, it depends on the scope, dear

Do I need a finished story or script?

No. We can start from a rough idea and help shape the story, flowchart and GDD with you.

How does payment work?

You'll have to pay a 50% down payment at first, and the 50% left after final work. For a large project, it could be split into milestones

How many revisions are included?

Two revision rounds per deliverable. Extra rounds are quoted separately.

How long does a project take?

Single assets take days to two weeks, a mini visual novel 1–2 months, a full game 3–6 months. It always depends on scope.

Who owns the rights?

You own the final assets after full payment. We may show the work in our portfolio unless you ask otherwise.

Can I hire you for just one service?

Absolutely. Illustration only, music only, a trailer only — whatever you need.

Which engine should I choose?

Check the Tech section for differences. Still unsure? Pick “Recommend” in the consultation form and we’ll suggest one.
